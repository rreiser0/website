Over the Fall 2025 semester, I had the great priveledge of partnering with CoBank as a part of my Business Analytics Capstone to financially support countless rural communities across the country. Three other students and I worked with CoBank's Vice President of Quantitative Risk with the goal of building a comprehensive, clean, and queryable database of balance sheet and risk data spanning the entire Farm Credit System and leading commercial banking institutions using Python and SQL. After successfully meeting that project scope with weeks to spare, our team additionally challenged ourselves to use our knowledge of econometrics and predictive analytics to answer several research questions, including but not limited to forecasting Total Loan Allowance across the Farm Credit System, understanding the macroeconomic differences of each region of the Farm Credit System, and understanding the business cycle differences between the Farm Credit System and traditional commercial banking. This project was an incredibly valuable opportunity that gave me real experience handling massive quantities of data and allowed me to finally apply all the analytical knowledge I have gained while studying Business Engineering at the Colorado School of Mines.
